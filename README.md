## Featured projects

### BPO Multi-Line Workforce Management & QA Dashboard
*Power BI · DAX · Power Query*
A workforce-management and quality-assurance dashboard for a contact-centre operation running three service lines. **50+ DAX measures** covering average handle time, occupancy, break/lunch/unavailable time, inbound/outbound call volume, and QA pass rates — organised into separate measure groups per line of business — let supervisors compare agent productivity and quality against targets in one view.
*Techniques: multi-fact modelling, KPI measure design at scale, per-line-of-business measure organisation.*

### Support Ticket Volume Forecasting & Capacity Planning
*Power BI · DAX*
A nine-page operational report analysing support-ticket volume by agent, source, board, and type, with resolution-time tracking, **required-vs-actual FTE modelling**, a **three-month volume forecast**, and **Pareto analysis** of ticket drivers — used for staffing and capacity-planning decisions.
*Techniques: time-series forecasting, Pareto (80/20) analysis, FTE/capacity modelling.*

### Product Complaints vs Sales Correlation Analysis
*Power BI · DAX*
An analysis of product-quality complaints against sales for a consumer-goods client, breaking complaints down by product and subject (taste, quality, preparation, texture) and **quantifying the correlation** between complaint volume and sales at product level, using waterfall and decomposition-tree visuals plus running-total and MoM DAX.
*Techniques: correlation analysis, decomposition tree, waterfall, running totals.*

### FMCG Sales Analysis — Top-N & Period Comparison  ·  *shareable*
*Power BI · DAX (what-if parameters)*
A fast-moving-consumer-goods sales analysis structured as answered business questions, using **what-if parameters for dynamic Top-N / Bottom-N ranking**, month-over-month comparison periods, and dedicated tooltip pages for drill context. Built on a public case-study dataset — included in this repo.

## DAX skills track  ·  *shareable*

A self-directed practice series on a generic sales dataset, building fluency in:

- **Time intelligence** — YTD, QTD, YoY %, MoM %, QoQ %, rolling 12-month averages, 30-day running totals
- **Dynamic ranking** — Top-N with and without rank totals; slicer-driven measure selection
- **Customer analytics** — lapsed-customer and common-customer logic, % of total
- The same calculation implemented multiple ways (e.g. YTD with and without `DATESYTD`) to understand the mechanics, not just the pattern

Included in this repo under `/practice`.
