# BPO Multi-Line Workforce Management & QA Dashboard

*Power BI · DAX · Power Query*

> Client engagement — confidential under NDA. Described by domain, method, and outcome only. No client name, data, or screenshots.

## Problem
A contact-centre operation running three distinct service lines needed a single view to manage agent productivity and quality against targets, instead of separate spreadsheets per line of business.

## What I built
A workforce-management and QA dashboard driven by **50+ DAX measures**, organised into separate measure groups per line of business, covering:

- Average handle time, occupancy, and after-call work
- Break, lunch, and unavailable time
- Inbound and outbound call volumes
- QA pass rates against target bands

## Techniques
- Multi-fact data model (agent info, roster, QA scores, activity) with a shared date dimension
- KPI measure design at scale, with per-line-of-business measure organisation
- Target-vs-actual visuals so supervisors can act at a glance

## Outcome
Supervisors compare agent productivity and quality across all three service lines in one place, replacing manual per-line reporting.
