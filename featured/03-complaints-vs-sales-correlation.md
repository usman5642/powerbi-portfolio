# Product Complaints vs Sales Correlation Analysis

*Power BI · DAX*

> Client engagement — confidential under NDA. Described by domain, method, and outcome only.

## Problem
A consumer-goods client wanted to know whether product-quality complaints were actually related to sales performance, and which products and issue types were driving complaint trends.

## What I built
An analysis breaking complaints down by product and by subject (taste, quality, preparation, texture) and **quantifying the correlation** between complaint volume and sales at product level.

## Techniques
- Correlation analysis between complaints and sales
- Decomposition-tree visual for interactive root-cause drilling
- Waterfall visuals plus running-total and month-over-month DAX to show how complaint volumes moved over time

## Outcome
Surfaced which specific products and issue types drove complaints, giving the client a fact-based view to feed into quality and product decisions.
