# Support Ticket Volume Forecasting & Capacity Planning

*Power BI · DAX*

> Client engagement — confidential under NDA. Described by domain, method, and outcome only.

## Problem
A support operation needed to understand ticket demand and plan staffing ahead of it, rather than reacting to volume spikes after they happened.

## What I built
A nine-page operational report covering:

- Ticket volume by agent, source, board, and type
- Resolution-time tracking
- Required-vs-actual **FTE modelling**
- A **three-month volume forecast**
- **Pareto analysis** of the ticket drivers behind most of the volume

## Techniques
- Time-series forecasting for forward capacity planning
- Pareto (80/20) analysis to focus effort on the biggest drivers
- FTE / capacity modelling linking demand to staffing

## Outcome
Gave the operation a forward view of demand and the staffing needed to meet it, supporting proactive capacity decisions.
